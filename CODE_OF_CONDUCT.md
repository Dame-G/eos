# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback constructively, and do not harass or demean anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly do.

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

To report a problem, contact the repository owner, [@Dame-G](https://github.com/Dame-G), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.

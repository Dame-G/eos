New `surface-hover` token: `surface-tint` pulled 3% toward `ink`, the selected nav item's fill. Hovered rows, menu items, nav items, tabs, pagination, calendar days, search results, segmented options and upload zones use it, so hover and selected fills match. It follows dark surfaces and `contrast: "high"` automatically.

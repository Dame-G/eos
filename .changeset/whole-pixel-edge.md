`--e-stroke-edge` is now a whole pixel (1px by default, and `strokes.hair` still scales it, rounded down). Controls draw their edge as a border with an identical outline laid over it, and a fractional width such as 1.5px was rounded differently for the two in Firefox, which showed as a doubled, thicker top and right edge on buttons.

`EStamp` and `ETag` have looks of their own, drawn from paper records instead of the usual badge and pill.

- `EStamp` is a rubber-stamp impression: the word in mono capitals, in the ink of its tone, inside a double rule. It is no longer filled in, so it does not read as a button. `final` is heavier, in the stamp's vermilion, and pressed on a little crooked.
- `ETag` is a paper tag: a small square-cornered label with a punched eyelet at the start, in place of the pill. A tag with an `icon` shows the icon where the eyelet would be.

`ETag` is a paper tag: a small square-cornered label with a punched eyelet at the start, in place of the pill. A tag with an `icon` shows the icon where the eyelet would be.

`ECombobox` draws its chosen options with `ETag`, so they match tags everywhere else. Its own chip styles are gone.

The character counter under `ETextField` and `ETextarea` stays on one line at the right edge of the field. A long hint wraps beside it instead of squeezing it, and on a narrow field the counter drops below the hint, still at the right.

`EStamp` has a thick side on its inline-start edge, in the darker shade of its own fill (muted grey for the neutral stamp), as a Notice has. It flips in a right-to-left page. The `final` stamp, which is a ring, has none.

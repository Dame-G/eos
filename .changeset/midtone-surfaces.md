`defineTheme` now gives readable action colours on mid-tone surfaces such as `#9e9e9e` and `#808080`. Before, the primary, secondary, danger and warning colours came out near white next to a white label. Each now tries both darker and lighter, and flips the label between black and white when that is the only way to hold the contrast. Themes that already passed keep their colours. The default `border` is a shade darker (`#7c8c93`) and derived borders are adjusted so the outline holds 3:1 on `surface-tint` as documented. `checkContrast` now follows `var(--e-name)` references, which it skipped before, and also checks `focus-ring`, `focus-field`, `muted` on `surface-tint` and `border` on `surface-tint`. Body text between about `#5c5c5c` and `#959595` cannot reach 7:1 in black or white, so a theme on those surfaces still reports `ink` on `surface` and `background`.

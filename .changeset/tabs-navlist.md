`ETabs` is horizontal only: the `orientation` and `sections` props are gone (use `ENavList` for a sidebar or outline). Every tab is ringed and flush with its neighbours, rounded only at the ends of the row, and the selected one is outlined in the primary colour with a thick bottom edge. Vertical `ENavList` items are divided by a hairline separator with space around it.
